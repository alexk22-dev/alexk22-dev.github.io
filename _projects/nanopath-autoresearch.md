**Paper:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10014) · [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=53dvsPErr1)

**Benchmark:** [labless.dev](https://labless.dev/nano-projects/nanopath) · [MedARC-AI/nanopath](https://github.com/MedARC-AI/nanopath)

- Drove 37% of the gain over baseline on [NanoPath v1](https://labless.dev/nano-projects/nanopath) (3rd-author paper, accepted at a NeurIPS 2026 workshop) and over 50% on [NanoBrain](https://labless.dev/nano-projects/nanobrain-smri) by building an LLM autoresearch agent with dual-branch search and anti-overfitting cycles.
- Cut config errors 80% and token use 15× by designing agent infrastructure (knowledge graphs, SLURM, worktree isolation).
